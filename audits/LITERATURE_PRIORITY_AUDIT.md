# Literature and priority audit

Three independent source-faithful searches were completed through August 6,
2026. They covered the authoritative problem statement and update genealogy,
publisher and DOI metadata, arXiv and mirrors, accessible MathSciNet and
zbMATH records, citation descendants, theses and repositories, public code and
data repositories, problem sites, forums, and formula-level searches.

No earlier publicly retrievable result was found that gives the exact
counterexample, a stronger counterexample subsuming it, the endpoint
classification, or the every-index construction. The safe classification is
therefore **apparently new, moderate confidence**.

The search also separated several false collisions:

- modern positive theorems concern the unit square `0<alpha,beta<=1`;
- the 2024 paper titled “On the general case of Brannan conjecture” proves a
  restricted unit-square region;
- historical statements about the degree-three case concern restricted
  parameter scopes and cannot be read as an all-positive theorem;
- Sturm sequences, root isolation, and CAD are known methods and are not
  claimed as new.

Absolute historical priority is not claimed. Complete older theses and
dissertations of Udaya C. Jayatilake, related seminar materials, restricted
older chapters and reports, and private or non-digitized sources were not all
retrievable.

# Mathematical audit

The proof candidate was frozen before adjudication. Three independent lanes
then performed:

1. a hostile line-by-line audit;
2. an independent reconstruction; and
3. a scope, certificate, and formalization-feasibility audit.

All three accepted the exact counterexample and the extension theorems after a
narrow repair round addressing source scope, witness-specific positivity, one
arithmetic transcription, and the terminal verdict label. The same three
lanes audited the exact repair delta and returned pass verdicts with no
remaining invalid inference.

The headline proof is symbolic. Its load-bearing steps are the direct
degree-three coefficient identity, the analytic-germ identity at `x=-1`, and
the exact rational comparison. The included Python program is a regression
check, not the proof.

Mathematical confidence: high. This is not a claim of human peer review or
journal acceptance.

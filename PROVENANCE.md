# Provenance

The investigation began from a source packet reproducing the generalized
question in Hayman--Lingham Problem 5.44. Three independent AI theorem runs
produced exact candidate counterexamples. The selected rational witness was
then frozen and subjected to independent mathematical audits, an authorized
repair-and-delta cycle, extension analysis, and three separate literature and
priority searches.

The public repository intentionally contains only the sanitized mathematical
record, audit conclusions, exact verifier, and reproducibility material.
Private conversation URLs, browser identifiers, account data, local machine
paths, and raw model-instance receipts are retained outside the public
surface.

No finite computation is load-bearing for the theorem. The pinned Lean project
kernel-checks only the exact rational witness, its strict reverse inequality,
and the gap `1/98`. It does not formalize analytic coefficient extraction, the
endpoint classification, or the every-index construction.

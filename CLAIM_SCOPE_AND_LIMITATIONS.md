# Claim scope and limitations

The proposed result negates the generalized all-positive-parameter assertion in
Hayman--Lingham Problem 5.44. It does not contradict or replace the established
theorem on `(alpha,beta) in (0,1]^2`, and it does not settle every other
one-parameter Brannan conjecture.

The exact witness and four stronger extensions passed three independent
mathematical audits after a narrow repair round. This does not establish
historical priority, peer review, publication acceptance, or formal
verification. Three documented literature searches found no publicly
retrievable identical or stronger counterexample through August 6, 2026, so
the result appears new with moderate confidence. Older inaccessible theses,
restricted chapters and reports, and non-digitized or unpublished work remain
a bounded historical uncertainty. Those statuses must remain separate in
every later manuscript, README, release, and DOI record.

The fixed-index Sturm/CAD reduction uses known methods and is not a novelty
claim. A historical sentence in Jayatilake's abstract must not be read as an
all-positive degree-three theorem; the paper's actual theorem is restricted to
`beta=1` and `0<alpha<1`.

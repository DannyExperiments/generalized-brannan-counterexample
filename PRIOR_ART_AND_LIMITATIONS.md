# Prior art and limitations

Three independent searches through August 6, 2026 found no publicly
retrievable identical or stronger counterexample to the literal all-positive
formulation. The exact endpoint classification, complete degree-three
unit-circle classification, and every-index construction were likewise not
found. The appropriate classification is `APPARENTLY_NEW`, with moderate
confidence; absolute historical priority is not claimed.

Known ingredients include coefficient extraction, the `x=-1` exponent
collapse, squared-modulus and trigonometric-polynomial reductions,
cosine/Chebyshev representations, Sturm sequences, root isolation, CAD, and
real quantifier elimination. The fixed-index decision architecture is known
methodology.

The principal unresolved source gaps are inaccessible or incompletely
retrieved older theses and dissertations by Udaya C. Jayatilake, related
seminar material, restricted older chapters and reports, and non-digitized or
unpublished work.

The result concerns the generalized all-positive question recorded by
Hayman--Lingham. It does not disprove the classical theorem for
`0<alpha,beta<=1`, and the all-positive formulation should not be attributed
unqualifiedly to Brannan.
